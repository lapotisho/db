1. Entities

Strong entities:

Patient
Doctor
Department
Appointment
Prescription
Specialization

Weak entity:

Room — because room number is only unique within a department. For example, Room 101 in Cardiology is different from Room 101 in Neurology.

3. Relationships and cardinalities
Patient — Appointment: 1:N
One patient can have many appointments.
Doctor — Appointment: 1:N
One doctor can have many appointments.
Patient — Doctor: M:N through Appointment
A patient can see many doctors and a doctor can see many patients.
Doctor — Specialization: M:N
A doctor can have multiple specializations and a specialization can belong to multiple doctors.
Department — Room: 1:N
One department can have many rooms.
Doctor — Prescription: 1:N
One doctor can prescribe many prescriptions.
Patient — Prescription: 1:N
One patient can have many prescriptions.
