2.Normalized relational schema

Student(StudentID PK, Name, Email)

Faculty(FacultyID PK, Name, Department, Email)

Club(ClubID PK, ClubName, Description, AdvisorID FK)

Membership(StudentID PK/FK, ClubID PK/FK, JoinDate, MembershipStatus)

OfficerPosition(PositionID PK, PositionName)

ClubOfficer(StudentID PK/FK, ClubID PK/FK, PositionID PK/FK, StartDate, EndDate)

Event(EventID PK, ClubID FK, EventName, EventDate, Description)

Attendance(EventID PK/FK, StudentID PK/FK, AttendanceStatus)

Room(RoomID PK, Building, RoomNumber, Capacity)

RoomReservation(ReservationID PK, EventID FK, RoomID FK, StartTime, EndTime)

Budget(BudgetID PK, ClubID FK, FiscalYear, AllocatedAmount)

Expense(ExpenseID PK, ClubID FK, ExpenseDate, Amount, Description)

3.Design decision

For officer positions, I could put PresidentID, TreasurerID, SecretaryID directly in the Club table. I chose a separate ClubOfficer table because a club can have different positions and it is easier to add new positions later.

4.Example queries
Find all students who are officers in the Computer Science Club, including their positions.
List all club events scheduled for next week with their room reservations.
Find the total expenses and allocated budget for each student club.
