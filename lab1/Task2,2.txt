2. Weak entity
OrderItem is a weak entity because it depends on an Order.
It does not have its own ID. It is identified by OrderID + ProductID.
For example, the same product can be in different orders, so we need the OrderID too.
3. Many-to-many relationship with attributes
There is an M:N relationship between Orders and Products.
One order can have many products and one product can be in many orders.
This relationship is represented by OrderItem.
OrderItem has its own attributes:
Quantity
PriceAtOrder
There is also an M:N relationship between Customers and Products through Reviews. Reviews have Rating, ReviewText and are associated with a specific customer and product.
